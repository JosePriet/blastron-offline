    Hey everybody! I’m Kyotosomo, leader of the guild Dark Phoenix! And in Synapse’s other game Tyrant Unleashed, I created a massive Tier List And Player Guide that hundreds of players use everyday and seeing how helpful it could be to the community; I wanted to create one for this game as well! If you want to check a unit’s stats you can click on it and it will take you to it’s page on Global Assault wiki. If you want more info in addition to this guide, you may want to check out Global Assault Wiki. Also keep in mind while using the Tier List that how powerful a unit is depends on the rest of the squad and each unit has its own purpose, so there isn't always a "best unit". So just use common sense and don't be afraid to go for what you think is best!

    Using the guide there are a few things you should know. Units are split into six categories based off the purpose they serve in battle and then the units are split into tiers based off how useful they are compared to other units. Within their section they are also organized from what units are generally most useful to generally least useful. Next to a unit will be a (T#) indicating a unit's in-game tier that will range from T1-T8, indicating how costly it is to upgrade to max level plus any assumptions one would make about higher tier units. Also next to a unit will be a "[X]" indicating whether a unit is:

  • [F2P] A unit that anybody can get for free from a normal supply crate.
  • [Semi-F2P] A unit one can get free, but at a lower chance than one who pays for crates.
  • [P2W] A unit which can only be gotten by buying a limited time crate with volt coins.
  • [Unique] A unit that can only be received one time from a special event or mission.

    • If you are having trouble finding a specific card you are looking for or a specific type of card, you can use your computer to find it by holding the “Control” key (Apple users must hold the “Command” key instead) and the “F” key. Then type your desired card into the text box that appears. And finally as mentioned before, this guide is split up into a Tier List and a Written Section, both of which are equally important.     This Month's Meta Analysis

    This section is for you newer players (and maybe veterans coming back from a break) to help guide you into building the right units and to understand what strategies are popular in the current meta game right now, so let's cut to the chase and get to it. Right now among F2P players due to a nerf decreasing ONI's stats, Eagles are dominating the meta and just like everyone else, all your resources should be dedicated to building more of them. Not only are they a hard counter to ONI (the other unit many people still love to spam), but they can do a good job of taking down Dirges and Eagles too because unlike defenders, you have control of where you can place units letting you can take full advantage of their Blitz. Against other F2P players you have no weakness and Eagles do a great job against a lot of P2W units as well.

    Speaking of, among P2W players having at least one Barrier All unit (Crusher, Ballista, or Whirlwind), one Rally All unit (Cerebral, Vulture, Myrmidon or Firefly), and one Mark All unit (Caledonia, Hunter, Zephyr, or Lobo) is a must. And units in addition to those P2W players like to spam are Badger (An instantly exploding grenade), Sandstorm (An impenetrable wall with ever raising attack), Razor Eagle (An instant hard hitter), and best of all by far; Defiance (An un-killable and high damage unit with Strike All). And of course anything else not mentioned but that you see is in God Class or Demigod Class is also a highly useful unit, especially with the introduction of T8 units. As a side note there has been a lot of Armor and Barrier in the meta lately with more to come so you might want to spice up your squad a bit with some Pierce. Units other than those mentioned have all sort of fallen out of the spotlight or continue to be not that popular. And that's about it for what the current meta is like!

    Squad Power, What It Means To You, And Calculating It

    Squad power, what is it and why should it matter to you? Well when you are about to face an opponent you want to know how strong you are and how strong they are relative to you. When people started playing this game squad strength was measured by Dominance, but this wasn't very accurate. Later Dark Phoenix came up with the system of adding up the sum of the tiers of all your units to measure squad strength which was definitely better, but still had its problems. Well the Devs finally implemented into their game a much more accurate system called Squad Power which takes into account the upgrade level of units and a squad with a wide range of different units. Squad Power is calculated like this:


    Each individual unit has its own Unit Power and when the Unit Power of every unit in your squad is added up, the resulting sum is your Squad Power. So:

    Unit 1 + Unit 2 + Unit 3 + Unit 4 + Unit 5 + Unit 6 + Unit 7 + Unit 8 = Squad Power
    Example: 34 + 37 + 40 + 43 + 46 + 46 + 54 + 64 = A Squad Power Of 364

    Okay, but how do you get get each individual unit's power in the first place? Well it's simple:

    (The Unit's Tier * 8 ) + Number Of Times Unit Upgraded = Individual Unit Power
    Example: (5 * 8) + 7 = An Individual Unit Power Of 47

    Properly being able to calculate Squad Power which is something very useful to know whether you're a new player trying to battle the right opponents or a veteran guild leader trying to recruit for their guild. Also two quick things I want to mention that some people have gotten confused on when calculating Squad Power. The tier of a unit is equal to the the number of stars it has plus an additional one or two tiers if it has been fused with itself once or twice. And the max upgrade level of a unit is equal to its tier plus two.


    I have also included a small set of Squad Power numbers so that when you see an enemy on the map you can estimate about how powerful they are and decide whether or not you want to engage them:

  • 304 Squad Power = Eight T4 Units
  • 340 Squad Power = Four T4 Units And Four T5 Units
  • 376 Squad Power = Eight T5 Units
  • 412 Squad power = Four T5 Units And Four T6 Units
  • 448 Squad Power = Eight T6 Units
  • 484 Squad Power = Four T6 Units And Four T7 Units
  • 520 Squad Power = Eight T7 Units
  • 536 Squad Power = Four T7 Units And Four T8 Units
  • 552 Squad Power = Eight T8 Units

    • With all this info I hope you are now able to successfully calculate Squad Power.

    Example End Game Squads

    As I have said many times here is no perfect squad which is one of the things that can make this game so fun for some people; it's up to each individual player to come up with their own unique squad. But because of this I'm sure many of you would probably be interested in knowing what an "End Game" squad would look like. Well I'm here to help by bringing you some examples of end game squads along with explanations for said unit choices. Well, here they are:

    A Quintessential End Game F2P Squad: Funeral Dirge (2), Jade ONI (2), and Razor Eagle (4) - "All End Game F2P squads will look something like this if they don't include any Semi-F2P units, although I could just as well see eight Razor Eagles being better. But I and many others I have talked to like the idea of including two ONIs and two Dirges for the sake of diversity and handling a greater range of scenarios. The ONIs can strip evade so that all the Eagles' Strikes will hit plus Strike All with Mark can solve some problems that Eagles can't against durable units with Armor."

    Kyo's Super Sexy Firestorm Of Death: Cinder Firefly (2), Cruel Hunter (2), Flaming Spitfire (2), and Smashing Crusher (2) - "With all the trends the top squads in the game seem to be following, I think I have a really unique target end game squad that could be a very good counter to most of these said top squads. Hunter has Mark All to boost the damage of all the units, Pierce so it can counter all the high Armor/Barrier we've been seeing lately, and Armor so it can act as a wall in the front to protect the squishier units in the back. Crusher also acts as a wall plus has Barrier All which provides the much needed extra protection to the other units on the field. It itself can obviously help dish out damage as well. Firefly helps boost the damage of all the other units on a whim plus is a very hard hitting Blitz unit not to mention that even after it is destroyed it will keep causing damage through Incendiary. Spitfire is the same except instead of the Rally All it too has Pierce for taking care of the massive amount of Armor and Barrier in the meta. I try to put Firefly behind Hunter and Spitfire behind Crusher too so that every row has a balance of stats and skills. Overall I think it's a rather quaint squad and will be very fun to use."

    The Very Definition Of Balance: Blazing Myrmidon (1), Cruel Hunter (1), Flaming Spitfire (1), Jade ONI (1), Raging Sandstorm (1), Razor Eagle (1), Smashing Crusher (1), and Spite Phantom (1) - "This squad incorporates many different units so that it can handle a plethora of different situations and simply bring a a little bit of everything to the table so that there's something for everyone. Every target all skill in the game is used and almost every single single target skill and damage modifier is used as well. Every unit has its specialties and this squad banks on that and despite not having a single theme, the diversity to handle all sorts of situations is what makes this a really good squad."

    Current Squad Of Dark Phoenix's N0Clu3 (The #1 Player In Global Assault): Blazing Myrmidon (1), Cruel Hunter (1), Depth Cerebral (1), Honey Badger (1), Razor Eagle (1), Slag Behemoth (1), and Solid Defiance (2) - "N0Clu3 has multiple of all the T6 and T7 quads in the game so he is constantly experimenting to find the best overall squad (although he does sometimes mold his squad to combat the specific opponent he is about to face), this is his current favorite he is playing around with. The Hunter, Cerebral, and Myrmidon are all for boosting damage plus the first two make good walls and Myrmidon can dish out a lot of damage and move around the field so it can hit more enemies. Behemoth is another good wall not to mention it can dish out quite a large amount of damage itself. Razor Eagle and Honey Badger provide some much needed Blitz, especially Badger whose instant Shrapnel can just devastate crowds. And finally Defiance who is still the best overall T7 unit in the game. The Strike All is superb with the Mark All, the high damage with Pierce is great, and the high health with Camouflage is awesome as well. Overall this is just a very well balanced and insanely strong squad."

    Other Tips For Beginners

  • First things first obviously you want to be as active as possible in the best way possible so that you can progress as fast as possible in the best way possible, well I recommend you do that by trying to fight other players who are below your Squad Power by at around 25 so that you get almost exclusively guaranteed wins resulting in more Platinum, more Scrap, and more Dominance. Only attempt missions if you know you can beat them, no point in wasting energy trying to beat an impossible mission that you will eventually be able to beat in one go later on. And speaking of which there are two missions where you get the rewards Raider and Fast Raider so I just want to warn you that you shouldn't bother making the unit Scav Raider until you get both those rewards since there is no point spending extra time and effort when you can make the unit at a much cheaper price.

  • It may be daunting when you are presented with all the different options for what to research and build, but I recommend that the absolute first thing you should do is focus on building a squad of ten Zeus before anything else. After that you should really focus on making sure that you have fully upgraded everything on your Helicarrier, I can't stress how important it is. Once you do that then you can focus on whatever you would like to and you should be fine (if you are a F2P player just remember that Grey Eagle is king right now).
