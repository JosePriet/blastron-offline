    zeuses are a good choice, you should aim on making at least 3 of them. i don’t know if you got kara, if so you should use it. strike all is most effective if units are marked, you can use kara and aurochs for that and maybe 1 or 2 galvanizers. blitzers are also a good idea, i would make 2 spitfires. they are the better choice compared to raiders because zeus and spitfire fuse into gray eagles (make them, they are much better than onis, trust me ;) ).
    a good squad imo is 4 zeuses, kara (or galv/auroch), galv (auroch), 2 spitfires.
    equip should be razor or ripper ammo for your spits, kara and galv (there are better things for galv but i wouldn’t waste research time on that), titanium platings for your zeuses and aurochs. if you have 4 zeuses and an auroch, put razor an one zeus and place it in the rear line.
