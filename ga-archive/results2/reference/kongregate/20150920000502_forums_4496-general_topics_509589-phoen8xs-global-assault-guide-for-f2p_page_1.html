    Overview

    This guide is intended for f2p players of Global Assault. It covers most of the topics and playing stages. But I do not pretend to know everything about the game. As most CCGs (if you call GA a CCG), card base and meta change constantly. It’s a living game so this guide can be out of date once it’s out. Please don’t blame the author. It’s all dev’s fault. :P

    Understanding the Game

    Before we go into the nitty gritty of which card to make and what deck to build, let’s get a grip of what is this game really about. At the core it’s a resources management game. When I say resources I really mean all of the following
    (1) Time – Let’s be honest, researches takes time (some people got it lucky and can skip some researches), upgrades take time, and fusion takes time. Then playing the battles takes time too.
    (2) Scraps – How do you get the max amount of scraps so your squad bay doesn’t wait in idle for scraps to upgrade units.
    (3) Cards – How do you know what cards to keep and where to get them.
    (4) Silver – How to maximize your income from battles and finish all daily objectives?
    (5) Attack – the amount of attacks per day is limited and you must use them wisely.

    General Strategy

    Think of GA as C&C. You build up your economy. Instead of building harvesters to harvest tiberium, you build up your army to farm the enemies. That’s your economy. So make sure you have the best harvesting deck, it could be very different to your Bounty Hunting deck.

    How to maximize your income? The current reward system is based on a number of factors:
    (1) SP reward is based on enemy SP. Enemy deck <200sp = 2 scraps, 200-399sp = 3 scraps and 400+sp = 4 scraps. So there is incentive to fight the high value enemies, if you can beat them.
    (2) Silver reward is based on enemy pod health and how much damage you do to it and enemy units. And if you loose or win the battle. Destroy the pod for max amount of silver

    Make sure you fully upgrade your Helicarrier. You will need it.

    Be selective of your unit/equipment research priorities. The most important researches for equipment are: razor ammo and titanium plating. For beginners it’s ripper ammo and steel plating.

    For unit research, the eagle tree is definitely your best friend. And if you have the materials, crusher/hunter/myrmidon are the so called holy trinity for f2p. The goal is to quad fusion each unit for max power.

    Don’t waste your effort on Oni. You will get two free from missions. And that’s all you need. (Thanks The_Atari_kid)

    Join a guild with active players. You want that extra boost from guild bonus. (Thanks The_Atari_kid) A good guild can give you many benefits such as bonus rewards, shortened research/upgrade time, guild war rewards and peer support. It will make your gaming experience much better.

    There are two ways to win a battle:
    (1) Destroy all the enemy units before turn 50 – overpower the opposition.
    (2) Destroy enemy command pod – pod rush. Blitz, blitz, rally all in empty lanes.
    If one doesn’t work try the pod rush, especially when enemy is more powerful than your own deck. Luck also plays a very large part in this game for f2p, so prey to your RNG God every night. j/k.

    Squad Development

    So we have come down to the details. Be mindful that Meta changes, BGE (background effect) changes and new units are added to the game continuously. So I can only tell you what I think is currently the best option. And this is definitely not the be all and end all. Use your own judgement and imagination. As f2p players you have to work with whatever units you are given by the RNG God.

    There are a lot other people who have fantastic ideas about deck builds etc. So what’s listed here is definitely not the only way forward. The only guide on the forum to avoid is the one with the most replies. It’s totally out of date and out of meta.

    For beginners

    I can’t really remember clearly what it was like in the beginning now. But Darius gets you through the first few missions. Fury and Auroch are the backbones of the squad. Kara provides the important rally all.

    Equip plating on armour units and razor on the rest. Kara can use motivational banner.

    Spitfire and Zeus are the next step, with Galvanizer for mark. Spitfire and Zeus later fuse into Eagle, which will be very important for your farming deck.

    There are some rare units such as jackrabbit and firefly which can not be fused from a lower tier. They are precious. Don’t waste them. You will need them. (thanks The_Atari_kids)

    If you are lucky enough to get enough material for Hunter then you probably don’t need to make Galvanizer. But Dirge+ can still be very useful.

    Crusher is also another unit that requires a lot of luck but eventually you will be able to make it. It’s the best f2p tanker with heavy armour and barrier all that keeps your army alive.

    Myrmidon can wait till later stages of game.

    Get some basic materials from league craters. It’s a good source of low level units and scraps(if they are useless).

    For Intermediate Players

    Blitz! Mark and strike! There are the key words.

    So you make a few eagles (with razor ammo), a dirge+ (razor ammo) or hunter(+)(titanium plating), and a SCAV. These are the mainstay of mid-level farming deck. If you manage to get Cerberus from mission then give yourself a pat on the back.

    It’s all about taking control of the rhythm of the battle. Take out one or two enemy units as soon as possible with SCAV or eagles. Keep two/three eagles in deck at all times or use a few SCAVs can be deadly too. Problem with SCAV is at high end their uses are very limited. Quad eagles can actually be used all the way to the end game deck. So personally I would not invest too heavily in SCAV.

    The goal to be a successful farmer is to have at least two eagle+, three would be better, SCAV, myrmidon+, hunter+ and crusher+ would complete the deck. A Dirge+ would be much appreciated too in early battle. This is a balanced farming deck.

    By now you should learn about short decks. 8-card deck is not the only way to play this game. A deck of 6 is more efficient than 8. 3 eagle+, dirge+, hunter+ and crusher+/myr+ can be a more deadly deck than the full roster. How it works? I leave it to you to explore. Sometimes even a 5-card deck is better for certain types of missions. And short squad has other perks associated with it. You need to discover it’s full potential on your own. I won’t spoil the fun for you.

    Some units like trident and sandstorm can give you surprises. And they can be good for specific raid missions. I have also seen player with 5 SCAVs and it does take a very good deck to defeat it without your pod getting sniped.

    If you want to do a pod rush deck, a deck that’s heavy in SCAVs(razor) or eagles(razor) and firefly+/@++ with banner would work well. Later the raid reward Halberd++ will also make pod rush a blitz.
    Don’t blame others for your own mistake in deck build. Observe what causes you problems, learn from them and adapt.

    Everyone has different play style. Learn what works for you and start to plan your own deck. Manage your research/upgrade carefully accordingly.

    For High Level Players

    So you already have a few quad T5 and building more with SP more than 430?

    At this stage you most likely know what your deck looks like. And you are probably doing raids and getting some good stuff from all the events. All I want to say is, some units may look innocent and useless, but sometimes there are creative ways of using them and they can be very handy.

    Do I really need to tell you what to make?

    The only tip is save your material and scraps. Be selective of what you make. The faster you can get new units in tune with meta change the better your deck is.

    Final words

    I hope you find this guide useful. Being f2p is not easy in this game. You need to spend more time and brain on it to be competitive. This is a game that takes months to get to the top, if you play smart and play hard.

    This game is about staying longer to collect materials and scraps. Eventually you will come out ahead. But don’t be disheartened by top p2w decks. They are rare and most are beatable.

    Finally, a shameless self promotion for Decepticon or AllSpark. The best coalition in da game!

    Cheers.

    I like the guide phoen8x, but just a couple things that I’d like to add/point out (feel free to ignore me phoen8x :) ). Join a guild as soon as you can. You get bonuses to silver, etc, and when there is a guild bounty, you have a chance at getting some units.

    I’ve said this before, and I’ll say it again (I agree with you regarding making Eagles): As a newish f2p player, you will be very tempted to make Onis out of your Zeuses because strike all will have served you well, but you will overall be better served by making Eagles.

    I always also like to point out be very careful with your ‘rare’ f2p units like Jackrabbit and Firefly. Both are 4 star units (so low drop rate in supply drops) and you have to decide how you want to use them. Do you make a quad Firefly or use them for Myrms (taking into account that you will need other units to fuse Myrms)? With the Jackrabbit, do you want to go the Ram route or the Hunter route?

    **On a side note, you might consider putting how to make some of those units in the ‘For beginners’ section. I know you can find how to make Myrm and Hunter in about 888 other threads, but it might be nice so someone doesn’t have to go looking.
